Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Westover

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush every year in hard-water areas.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet that same buildup, neglected through a few more years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and burn out the lower element on an electric model, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Common Water Heater Problems in Westover

Long before a tank floods a Westover garage, it usually shows warning signs. Watch for rumbling and popping while the tank heats, brownish hot water, a sulfur smell from the hot tap, moisture around the base, or showers that run cold sooner every month. Those point to heavy sediment, a depleted anode, a weak element or burner, and early corrosion, and caught early, they're usually an affordable repair or a planned replacement. Ignored, they end in a failed tank and water on the floor. A dripping T&P valve deserves special attention: it means the tank is seeing too much pressure or temperature, and it should be checked promptly. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so the small problem never gets the chance to grow.
